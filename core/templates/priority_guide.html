Priority Guide

Understand how fairness, safety, and fast resolutions work.

What determines priority?
Type of Issue

Electrical, Water, and Lifts are naturally critical and handled faster.

People Affected

Issues affecting an entire block get higher priority than single flats.

Safety Risk

Safety-related issues are always treated as urgent emergencies.

Evidence Provided

Photos help us understand the issue instantly and resolve it quicker.

Priority Levels & Service Level Agreements (SLA)
Critical Priority
Safety risks or essential service loss
Within 2 Hours
High Priority
Major disruptions to standard living
Within 8 Hours
Medium Priority
Affects daily routine but not critical
Within 24 Hours
Low Priority
Minor inconvenience or aesthetic
Within 48 Hours

Why You Don't Need to Worry

  • You don't need to call or remind us.
  • The system tracks resolution time automatically.
  • Escalation happens instantly if delayed.
  • You’ll be notified of every status update.
