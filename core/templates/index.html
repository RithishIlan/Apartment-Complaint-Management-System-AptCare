Smart Priority Engine

Our system automatically calculates complaint urgency based on multiple factors: safety impact, number of affected residents, evidence provided, and type of issue. Critical problems like water leaks or electrical issues get immediate attention.

Neglect Detection & Alerts

Complaints approaching deadlines or being ignored trigger automatic escalation. Managers receive alerts and if still unresolved, the issue gets elevated to higher authorities — ensuring accountability at every step.

Real-time Tracking Dashboard

Residents can track their complaint status in real-time. See exactly where your issue stands in the resolution pipeline, who's responsible, and estimated completion time. Complete transparency eliminates uncertainty.
